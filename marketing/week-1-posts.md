## Day 2 — Education: why targeting types need separate campaigns

**LinkedIn**
> A mistake we see in almost every account audit:
>
> Auto targeting, keywords and product targets all crammed into one campaign.
>
> Two problems:
> 1. Amazon won't accept keywords inside an auto campaign — a bulk upload like that fails.
> 2. Even when it's built manually, you can't control budget or bids per strategy. Discovery spend eats your proven keywords' budget.
>
> The fix: one campaign per targeting type.
> • Auto → discovery
> • Keyword → performance
> • Product targeting → conquest and defense
>
> Our free bulk sheet generator splits these automatically. Link in comments.

## Day 5 — Education: negative keyword levels

**LinkedIn**
> Campaign-level or ad-group-level negative keywords? It matters more than most sellers think.
>
> • Campaign level: the term is blocked across every ad group in the campaign. Use it for terms that are never relevant ("free", "used", competitor brands in discovery campaigns).
> • Ad group level: blocked only in that ad group. Use it to stop two ad groups competing for the same search term.
>
> In a bulk sheet these are different row types — mix them up and the upload fails.
>
> Our free generator handles both. Link in comments.

**X**
> Campaign-level negative = blocked everywhere in the campaign.
> Ad-group-level negative = blocked in that ad group only.
>
> Use campaign level for never-relevant terms, ad group level to stop your own ad groups competing.
